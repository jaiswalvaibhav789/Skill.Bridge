## Slide 3: Technical Approach, Architecture & Algorithms

### 🎨 Visual Layout
- **Left Column (3-Tier Enterprise Topology):**
  - **Client Presentation Tier:** React 18, Vite 5, Tailwind CSS, SVG Radar Visualizations, Client-side session timeout & route guards.
  - **Gateway & Security Tier:** Helmet.js, strict CORS, express-rate-limit, UUID distributed request tracing (`X-Request-ID`), NoSQL injection sanitizers.
  - **Application Service Tier:** Node.js 20 LTS, Express REST API, 10 modular micro-services, JWT Bearer Token rotation, 5-Role RBAC.
  - **Data Persistence Tier:** MongoDB 7.0+ Replica Set, 18 Mongoose Schemas, compound indexes (<50ms query latency), append-only audit trail.
- **Right Column (Core Mathematical Formulations):**
  - **1. 5-Factor Weighted Compatibility Score:**
    $$\mathbf{Score} = \min\Big(100, \max\big(0, \text{round}(0.50 \cdot S + 0.20 \cdot E + 0.15 \cdot C + 0.10 \cdot P + 0.05 \cdot L)\big)\Big)$$
    *(S: Diagnostic Skill 50% | E: Academic Degree/CGPA 20% | C: Career Role 15% | P: Practical Projects 10% | L: Location 5%)*
  - **2. 5-Tier Deficit Vector & Gap Categorization:**
    $$\text{Deficit}(k_i) = \text{TargetThreshold}(k_i) - \text{VerifiedScore}(k_i)$$
    *(Critical [$\ge 30\%$], High [$20\text{–}30\%$], Medium [$10\text{–}20\%$], Low [$1\text{–}10\%$], Satisfactory [$\le 0\%$])*
  - **3. Deterministic Cryptographic Credential Signature:**
    $$\mathbf{Credential\ Hash} = \mathbf{SHA256}(\text{studentId} : \text{skillId} : \text{score} : \text{endorsedBy} : \text{timestamp})$$

## 🎯 Jury Q&A Cheat Sheet (Top 8 Defense Questions)

### Q1: "How do you prevent candidates from fabricating skills or cheating during assessments?"
**Answer:**  
> *"SkillBridge implements a multi-tier defense: First, skills are never self-declared text; they require passing a timed diagnostic assessment with randomized question sequencing, anti-tab switch detection, and instant timeout locks. Second, upon passing, the system calculates a SHA-256 cryptographic hash combining the student ID, skill ID, score, timestamp, and institutional authority ID. Any tampering in the database or client invalidates the hash immediately, ensuring 100% cryptographic integrity."*

### Q2: "Why can't companies just use LinkedIn or Internshala for Ayush hiring?"
**Answer:**  
> *"LinkedIn and Internshala are generic, keyword-based boards. They cannot evaluate whether an Ayush graduate understands Schedule T GMP sterilization protocols, Classical Ayurvedic formulation standards, or Pharmacovigilance. Recruiters still have to screen hundreds of unvetted resumes. SkillBridge provides domain-specific diagnostic assessments, pre-computes a 5-factor mathematical match score, and gives employers pre-screened, verified talent ready to join on day one."*

### Q3: "How does the 5-Factor Recommendation Engine work mathematically?"
**Answer:**  
> *"Our compatibility score formula weights 5 distinct dimensions summing strictly to 100%: Diagnostic Skill coverage (50%), Academic Degree & CGPA compliance (20%), Target Career Role alignment (15%), Practical project certifications (10%), and Regional location preference (5%). This produces an explainable score between 0 and 100%, complete with human-readable rationale badges for both candidates and recruiters."*

### Q4: "How does the platform align with the National Education Policy (NEP 2020)?"
**Answer:**  
> *"NEP 2020 emphasizes three core pillars: credit-bearing internships, multidisciplinary learning, and academia-industry research. SkillBridge directly executes this through our 6-stage internship state machine with weekly verified mentor logs, our remedial course enrollment engine that rewards modular micro-credentials, and our Faculty R&D portal facilitating industry-funded sabbaticals and joint research."*

### Q5: "How does the Ministry of Ayush benefit from this platform?"
**Answer:**  
> *"The Ministry gets a nationwide macro-telemetry dashboard. Currently, the Ministry lacks real-time data on whether Ayush graduates possess the skills required by modern pharmaceutical plants. SkillBridge visualizes regional skill gaps via heatmaps, tracks nationwide placement absorption rates, and highlights syllabi deficits to help the NCISM and NCH update national curricula."*

### Q6: "Is the system tested and production ready?"
**Answer:**  
> *"Yes! We have an enterprise test suite with 97 automated tests across 10 distinct suites covering authentication, role-based access, recommendation algorithms, skill-gap deficit calculations, and portfolio hashing. All 97 tests pass with 100% success rate, supported by 18 indexed Mongoose schemas and Docker multi-stage containerization."*

### Q7: "What is your database performance strategy for pan-India scale?"
**Answer:**  
> *"We utilize MongoDB 7.0+ replica sets with strategic compound and sparse unique indexing (e.g., compound indexes on `[student, opportunity]`, `portfolioSlug`, and `requestHeaders`). Standard REST queries respond in under 50 milliseconds. The system is stateless with JWT tokens and can horizontally scale behind an Nginx reverse proxy or AWS ECS cluster."*

### Q8: "How does your Faculty Collaboration module work?"
**Answer:**  
> *"Unlike standard placement portals, we include professors as primary stakeholders. Faculty members can publish clinical research capabilities, respond to corporate consulting RFPs (Requests for Proposals), and apply for industry sabbaticals in Ayush herbal manufacturing facilities. This directly solves the academic research-to-commercialization bottleneck."*
