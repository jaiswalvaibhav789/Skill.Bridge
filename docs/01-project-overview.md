# 01. Project Overview: Academia–Industry Collaboration Portal

> **Project Name:** SkillBridge Enterprise (Academia–Industry Collaboration Portal)  
> **Target Theme:** Smart Automation & Educational Technology  
> **Lifecycle:** Assess → Analyze → Learn → Apply → Collaborate → Intern → Get Placed → Track Progress  

---

## 1. Executive Summary
The **Academia–Industry Collaboration Portal** is a centralized, secure, multi-tenant digital platform engineered to bridge the gap between academic curricula and evolving industrial requirements. It integrates standardized multi-dimensional skill assessments, quantitative skill gap analysis, personalized upskilling pathways, an internship marketplace, recruitment pipeline management, faculty-industry collaboration, and role-specific executive analytics.

## 2. Core Stakeholders
1. **Students:** Undergraduate and postgraduate students seeking objective evaluation of their market readiness, clear actionable steps to close skill deficits, and high-compatibility internship and placement opportunities.
2. **Industry & Corporate Recruiters:** Enterprises seeking to eliminate candidate screening friction through skill-verified applicants, pre-calculated role-fit scores, custom internship postings, and structured intern progress evaluation.
3. **Faculty & Academicians:** University professors and departmental heads seeking corporate consulting opportunities, joint research initiatives, Faculty Development Programs (FDPs), and student mentorship workflows.
4. **Institutions & Placement Cells:** Academic administrators requiring institutional visibility into placement velocity, curriculum deficiencies, and regional skill demands to maintain accreditation standards and modernize syllabi.
5. **Super Administrators / Ministry:** Overseers requiring nationwide macro analytics to guide regional workforce policy and academic-industry alignment.

## 3. End-to-End System Lifecycle
```
[Student Registration]
        ↓
[Standardized Skill Assessment (MCQs, Aptitude, Soft Skills)]
        ↓
[Empirical Skill Profile Generated (Radar / % Breakdown)]
        ↓
[Skill Gap Analysis vs. Target Career Role (Critical / High / Med / Low)]
        ↓
[Personalized Learning Recommendations (Courses, Certifications, FDPs)]
        ↓
[Algorithmic Opportunity Matching (Internships & Full-Time Jobs)]
        ↓
[Application Submission with Match Score & Missing Skill Transparency]
        ↓
[Corporate Screening, Shortlisting & Status Pipeline Tracking]
        ↓
[Active Internship Tracking (Weekly Milestones & Mentor Evaluation)]
        ↓
[Completion Certificate Issued & Portfolio Automatically Updated]
```

## 4. Key Differentiating Features
* **Empirical Skill Intelligence:** Replaces subjective resume keywords with verifiable diagnostic test scores.
* **Deterministic Compatibility Scoring:** A transparent 5-factor weighted algorithm ($Score \in [0, 100\%]$) removing recruiter guesswork.
* **Closed-Loop Upskilling:** Directly connects every identified skill gap to specific remediation courses and programs.
* **Tamper-Proof Digital Portfolio:** Provides a verifiable public showcase of validated competencies, project milestones, and endorsements.
